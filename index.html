About Apex Lark

Apex Lark is a registered company offering internships in AI/ML, Data Analytics, Data Science and Full-Stack Development. All our internships are fully remote.

We help students and freshers move from learning to doing. Interns get tasks, real projects, career guidance and support with their job-search problems, so they finish with skills and work they can show.

We are registered under Udyam and on the National Career Service (NCS) portal.
